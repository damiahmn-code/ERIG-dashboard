12 Key Research Focus Areas (Scholarship of Learning & Teaching)

Core Grant Objectives (Section 1.1)

1. Educational Innovation Discover new ideas, theories, concepts, and innovations elevating teaching & learning quality.
2. High-Impact Publications Generate outputs in high-quality peer-reviewed journals of international repute (Q1/Q2).
3. Pump-Priming Funding Execute proof-of-concept studies to gather preliminary data for external research grant bids.
4. Cross-Boundary Synergy Collaborate across Sunway schools, partner institutions, and research-intensive universities.

Important Provision Rules & Separate Funds

Conferences & Workshops (VOTE 29000): Not covered by ERIG; use the Conference Participation Support Fund. Workshops/training without paper presentations are prohibited.
Publication Fees & Field Assistants (VOTE 29000): Publication fees follow the Publication Support Scheme. Field Assistants are capped at RM10/hr or RM2,500/mo (submit Hiring Form to EEAS prior).
Equipment Ownership (VOTE 35000): Must justify why existing University gear cannot be used. Purchased items remain Sunway University property (tagged by ITS/Facilities); maintenance covered only during project term.

Post-Award Governance & Reporting Timeline (Sections 3 & 4)

Late reports suspend funding & disqualify future internal applications.
Prior to Start

Ethics Clearance

Obtain approval from Sunway University Ethics Committee before starting research (Sec. 4.3).

Within 3 Months

Scope & Virement

Deadline to submit Scope Revisions (RC/DD endorsed) or Budget Virement forms (DD R&S endorsed) to EEAS.

First 6 Months

50% Spend Rule

Project must utilize at least 50% of allocated funding within the first 6 months of award (Sec. 2.2).

End of Month 6

Mid-Term Report

Submit Progress Report for Internal Research Grant Scheme form to EEAS at the 6-month mark (Sec. 4.1).

End & +3 Months

Final & 2-Page Report

Submit Final Report at end, plus a 2-page summary (200-word abstract, photos, final budget) & presentation within 3 months.

Team & PI Change Protocols (Sec. 3.2–3.3)

Change in Project Leader (PI): Requires 1 month advance notice via Change of Membership Form (Deputy Dean R&S endorsed) for sabbatical/unpaid leave, resignation, transfer, or illness. Must nominate a Sunway co-researcher; otherwise project terminates & Final Report is due.
Change in Team Membership: Submit Change of Membership Form (Deputy Dean R&S endorsed) to EEAS as soon as possible.

Mandatory Publication Acknowledgement & Affiliation (Sec. 4.4)

Update the Centre for Research Management (CRM) when post-project papers are published.
